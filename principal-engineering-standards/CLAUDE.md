# Principal Engineering Instructions

Read `SKILL.md` first and follow its workflow. Load only the relevant files under `references/`.

## Working rules

- Clarify business outcome, constraints, scale, data sensitivity, and regulatory scope before committing to a design.
- Prefer simple, reversible decisions. Record expensive or hard-to-reverse choices as ADRs.
- Never fabricate requirements, benchmark results, legal conclusions, cloud limits, or external URLs.
- Treat security, privacy, accessibility, operability, and cost as design inputs.
- Produce numbered Mermaid diagrams for non-trivial architecture, sequence, data-flow, and ERD work.
- Define measurable acceptance criteria and test evidence.
- Use current official product documentation when behavior, support, limits, or compliance claims may have changed.
- Flag assumptions and residual risks explicitly.
